About

Research scientist working on optimization of large personalized models.

I work at the intersection of large personalized models and music recommendation. At Spotify, I build systems that shape music and podcast experiences on a platform serving hundreds of millions of listeners. My research takes a data-centric approach to training and adaptation, through targeted data selection, continual learning, and optimization recipes that help models improve at scale.

A little more about me

I’m from Montevideo, Uruguay, where I studied electrical engineering. I also interned at CERN, building visualization tools for large particle-collision datasets in the CMS Open Data initiative. I later studied statistics and electrical engineering at the University of Pennsylvania, focusing on optimization theory, and was a visiting student at Stanford’s Kundaje Lab, where I worked on BasepairModels, a Python library for regulatory genomics analysis.

Leadership and community

I played for Uruguay’s national water polo team. I’ve also helped build communities around research and education: I organized the NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Constrained Optimization for Machine Learning and serve on the Junior Board of ReachingU, a nonprofit advancing educational opportunity in Uruguay.
